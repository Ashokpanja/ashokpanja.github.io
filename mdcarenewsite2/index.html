MDCare AI

Smarter Documentation. Better Care.

MDCare AI helps clinicians reduce documentation time with AI-powered voice dictation and intelligent clinical note creation. Simply speak naturally during the patient encounter, and MDCare AI helps transform the conversation into structured documentation for new consultations, follow-ups, SOAP notes, HPI, assessments, plans, and more—so providers can spend less time typing and more time caring for patients.

Key Highlights:

🎙️ Voice Dictation 📝 Multiple Note Types ⚡ Faster Documentation 👩‍⚕️ More Time for Patients
